## 📖 About

Ender Magnolia: Bloom in the Mist by Binary Haze Interactive (2025) is the sequel to Ender Lilies. You play as Lilac, a tuner awakening in an underground world overrun by corrupted Homunculi. Its tight action-RPG combat and gorgeous art direction build on everything Ender Lilies established.
